# Reserve an SSMLink adapter

The SSMLink adapter is a small Bluetooth box that plugs into the **yellow 9-pin Select Monitor
connector** on 1990–1996 Subaru engine computers. With the free SSMLink app for iPhone and
Android it shows live engine data, gauges and trouble codes, and records drives.

Ready-made adapters are in development. Reserve one now and you'll be first in line when the
first batch is built.

**[Reserve an adapter by email](mailto:nickma@gmail.com?subject=SSMLink%20adapter%20reservation&body=Hi%2C%20please%20reserve%20an%20SSMLink%20adapter%20for%20me.%0A%0AName%3A%20%0AVehicle%20(year%2C%20model)%3A%20%0AEngine%20(e.g.%20EJ22%2C%20EJ18%2C%20EG33)%3A%20%0AStock%20Subaru%20or%20conversion%20(e.g.%20Vanagon)%3A%20%0AiPhone%20or%20Android%3A%20%0ACountry%3A%20%0A)**

Reserving is free and isn't a commitment to buy. Nothing is charged now. When adapters are
ready you'll get one email with the price and ship date, and you decide then.

## What you get

- A plug-in adapter for the yellow Select Monitor connector, ready to use: no soldering
- Power from the diagnostic connector, or from a cigarette-lighter plug if your harness
  doesn't carry 12 V on that pin
- The SSMLink app, free on the App Store and Google Play

## Will it work with my car?

SSMLink talks to Subaru engine computers from about **1990 to 1996** that have the yellow
Select Monitor connector, including:

- Legacy, Impreza and SVX from those years
- Subaru-powered conversions such as **EJ22 swaps in VW Vanagons**, if the swap harness kept
  the yellow connector

It doesn't use the OBD-II port found on later cars. If your car only has OBD-II, SSMLink isn't for it.

Not sure? Open the app, tap **Try the demo ECU** on the Live tab to see every screen with
simulated data, and mention your car in the reservation email. I'll check it before you buy.
